Certificate PDF Builder
=======================

1. Double-click CertificatePDFBuilder.exe (nothing to install).
   If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info > Run anyway.

2. Click "+ Add files..." (or "Add folder...") and choose the device Excel files.
   The table shows a tick or a cross for each of the 4 tabs.
   Use the arrow buttons or "Sort A-Z" to set the order.

3. Choose where to save, then click "Create PDFs". You get two files:
     Certificates.pdf           - ECG.certificate + NIBP.certificate of every device
     Pass-Fail Test Sheets.pdf  - ECG.pass-fail + NIBP.pass-fail of every device

Needs Microsoft Excel or the free LibreOffice (https://www.libreoffice.org) on the PC.
If your tabs have different names, click "Tab names..." to change them.
Your Excel files are only read, never changed.
